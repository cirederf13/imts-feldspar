DESCRIPTION:  
Greenland Ice Core Chronology 2005 (GICC05) 
Released 27 November 2006.
Main GICC05 time scale file, covering the entire section available 
in 20-year resolution (0-41.76 ka b2k, where b2k == before A.D. 2000).

NGRIP and GRIP d18O data provided:
NGRIP d18O values on GICC05 time scale back to 41.76 ka b2k (years relative to A.D. 2000).
GRIP d18O values on GICC05 time scale back to 32.44 ka b2k (years relative to A.D. 2000).

All dates are given using the b2k notation (years relative to A.D. 2000).

Holocene data for NGRIP1, NGRIP2, GRIP, and DYE-3 can be found in a separate file, 
named GICC05_Holocene_20y_27nov2006.txt obtainable from www.icecores.dk or from 
IGBP PAGES/World Data Center for Paleoclimatology.

The concept of maximum counting error (column no. 6) is introduced in 
Rasmussen et al. (Journ. Geophys. Res., 2006). In a standard deviation context, 
the maximum counting error should be regarded as 2 sigma as discussed in 
Andersen et al. (see reference list).

Age and depth values refer to the bottom of each interval. 
Thus the NGRIP depth interval 14.15-20.4 m corresponds to the time interval 40 - 60 b2k, 
in which the NGRIP d18O value is -35.11 permille.

The NGRIP1 core reaches down to a depth of 1372 m. 
The NGRIP2 core (drilled 20 meters away from the NGRIP1 core) reaches from surface to 
bedrock, but high-resolution measurements have only been carried out downwards from 1346 m. 
In the 26 m overlap zone, the cores are offset by 0.43 m on average, probably due to 
uncertainties in the logging procedure (see Schøtt Hvidberg et al., Ann. Glac. 35, 2002). 
Thus, the same features appear at larger depths in the NGRIP1 than in the NGRIP2 core. 
In this file, NGRIP1 depths are used until 9820 b2k, and NGRIP2 depths are used below this.

In the Holocene, GRIP is dated year by year. Below 11700 b2k the NGRIP based time scale 
has been transferred to GRIP depths by linear interpolation between volcanic match points 
(markers shown on Fig. 10 in Rasmussen et al. (2006) down to 14.7 ka b2k and in 
Rasmussen et al. (2007) below this). 
These match points are available for download at http://www.icecores.dk.
The maximum counting error in column 6 does not include uncertainty from this interpolation.

For more data downloads, check also the data page at www.icecores.dk
